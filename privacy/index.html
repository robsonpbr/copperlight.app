Privacy

What you type stays on your Mac.

Copperlight has no account, no analytics and no crash reports, and it uses no AI service. It only reaches out when you ask for something that lives elsewhere, like a stock price or the weather, and then only with what that answer needs. Here is all of it.

Every server it contacts

Searches and links open in your browser, so they’re between you and the site you chose. Everything Copperlight asks for itself is below. The source is open, so you can check every line.

What it keeps

Everything is in ~/.config/copperlight/ on your Mac, and nowhere else: your settings, notes, clipboard history (readable only by you), what it learned about what you open, and cached currency rates and app descriptions. Copies made in password managers are never recorded, and a link you copy loses its tracking (utm_, fbclid). Settings has a Forget button for what it learned; deleting the folder removes everything. One thing is kept outside it: your Todoist key, if you save one, in your Mac’s keychain, until you forget it in Settings.

Contacts, browser tabs, reminders and events are read when you open the launcher and kept in memory only. codes reads only the last 15 minutes of Messages, when you type it. A Wi-Fi password is shown only after macOS asks for your Mac’s password, and kept in memory for 10 minutes.

Turning things off

Any feature can be turned off in Settings › Features, or in config.toml. Off means off: no rows, no background work, no network, no permission asked.

Questions

Does Copperlight collect any data about me?

No. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reports and no AI service. What you type stays on your Mac, and what it keeps is in ~/.config/copperlight/ on your Mac, apart from a Todoist key you save, which stays in your Mac’s keychain.

Does Copperlight work offline?

Yes. Apps, files, math, units, clipboard history, snippets, notes, windows and translation all work without a connection. Stocks, the weather, Wikipedia, currency rates and the speed test need one.

Why does Copperlight ask for Accessibility?

To paste into the app you were in, move windows, search menus and read the text you selected. Without it, a paste copies instead.
